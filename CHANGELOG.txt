v1.0.0 (2026-09-29) - First public release
- Full rebrand to Forever Dungeon Tools: new logo, minimap icon, window title and tooltip
- Dungeon planner for the Classic and Classic Forever dungeons (Ruins of Lordaeron, Hall of Thanes, ...)
- Opening the world map inside a dungeon opens the FDT map on your floor instead (Settings: can be disabled);
  the map key toggles it closed again
- Live player arrow and group member dots on the FDT map where the game exposes positions
- Restricted dungeons (positions hidden, e.g. Ruins of Lordaeron): red "you are here" dot on your current named zone
- Mob tracker (/fdt track on|off|apply|reset|status): records kills, levels, elites and bosses per zone while you play,
  then places the killed packs on the map, even in dungeons where the game hides mob names and positions
- /fdt track name <name> teaches the tracker the name of the last "Unknown mob" pack; learned names are reused
- /fdt where prints map/position diagnostics
- Fixes: secret-value errors in restricted dungeons, aura access taint error, map not opening on first use,
  raw locale keys in the UI, duplicate library loading
- All new texts translated in the 11 supported languages
